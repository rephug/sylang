The prefix M0.1: represents version core-v0.1. A predicate is p(SUBJECT,RELATION,OBJECT,POLARITY,TENSE,ASPECT,EVIDENCE), in exactly that positional order. SUBJECT and OBJECT are JSON-quoted strings. Relation: s=see,h=help,c=contain. Polarity: +=positive,-=negative. Tense: p=past,n=present,f=future. Aspect: s=simple,g=progressive,c=completed. Evidence: u=unspecified,d=direct,r=reported,i=inferred. A conditional is i(CONDITION,CONSEQUENCE), containing nodes recursively. The root node is statement.
