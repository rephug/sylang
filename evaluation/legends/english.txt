The prefix Sylang core-v0.1: supplies the version. A predicate consists of an evidence prefix, a JSON-quoted subject, a singular verb phrase, a JSON-quoted object, and a period. Evidence prefixes: Without specified evidence, = unspecified; By direct observation, = direct; By report, = reported; By inference, = inferred. Relations use see/sees/saw/seeing/seen, help/helps/helped/helping/helped, and contain/contains/contained/containing/contained. Simple aspect: present sees, past saw, future will see. Progressive: present is seeing, past was seeing, future will be seeing. Completed: present has seen, past had seen, future will have seen. Equivalent help/contain forms follow these patterns. Negation uses does not see/did not see/will not see for simple, is not seeing/was not seeing/will not be seeing for progressive, and has not seen/had not seen/will not have seen for completed. Other phrases are positive. A conditional is If [CONDITION] then [CONSEQUENCE]. with recursively nested nodes. The root node is statement; no pragmatic inference is allowed.
