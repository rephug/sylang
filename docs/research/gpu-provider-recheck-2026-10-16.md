# GPU provider verification for the Qwen3-0.6B/1.7B LoRA pilot (retrieved 2026-10-16 unless stated)

All official pages were fetched 2026-10-16. Lightning AI's site is a JavaScript shell; its figures were obtained by rendering the official pages through a read-only proxy (r.jina.ai) and are marked [rendered]. Hourly figures in brackets are conversions of published per-second rates.

## 1. Modal (modal.com/pricing, /docs/guide/budgets, /docs/guide/gpu, /docs/guide/volumes, /docs/guide/timeouts, /docs/guide/preemption, /docs/guide/cron, /docs/guide/apps, /docs/examples/long-training)

GPU, per second: T4 $0.000164 [$0.59/h]; L4 $0.000222 [$0.80/h]; A10 $0.000306 [$1.10/h]; L40S $0.000542 [$1.95/h]; A100 40 GB $0.000583 [$2.10/h]; A100 80 GB $0.000694 [$2.50/h]; H100 SXM5 $0.001097 [$3.95/h]; H200 $0.001261; RTX PRO 6000 $0.000842; B200 $0.001736; B300 $0.001972.
CPU $0.0000131 per physical core-second [$0.047/core-h], minimum 0.125 cores; memory $0.00000222/GiB-s [$0.008/GiB-h], minimum 128 MiB; "charged for whichever is higher: the amount requested or the amount used". Sandboxes/Notebooks CPU and memory 3x; GPU same as Functions.
Starter plan: "$0 + compute / month", "$30 / month free credits"; FAQ: "Starter has no monthly fee and includes $30 of compute (GPUs, CPUs, Memory) per month. You're billed when you exceed that"; "Included usage applies to compute: Functions, Sandboxes, and Notebooks ... Volumes include up to 1 TiB free"; pay-as-you-go. Starter: 3 seats, 100 containers, 10 GPU concurrency, log retention 1 day. GPU docs: "Using a GPU requires having a valid payment method on file." Credit rollover not stated.
Budgets: Workspace budget = "a monthly cap for total Workspace usage across the Workspace" before credits, "the hard outer cap"; Spend limit = monthly cap on out-of-pocket charges; "When the spend limit is reached, Modal stops workloads that would incur additional out-of-pocket charges"; default spend limit = usage limit minus credits; set at /settings/usage by Owners/Managers.
Volumes: $0.09/GiB/month, "includes 1 TiB / mo free"; deleted data may bill for up to four days. Egress $0.04/GiB with 1 TiB/month free on Starter. Region selection 1.15–1.75x; "Non-preemptible execution 3x base prices".
Runtime: default timeout 300 s, settable up to 24 hours; `modal run --detach`; `.spawn()`; Cron requires `modal deploy`. "All Modal Functions are subject to preemption by default"; `nonpreemptible=True` "not supported for GPU Functions". Long-training example: checkpoint to a Volume, retries, resume from `last.ckpt`.
Awkwardness for a plain PyTorch script: wrap as `@app.function(gpu=..., image=..., volumes=..., timeout=...)`; data on Volumes visible only after commit; no SSH/persistent VM; GPU functions preemptible with no opt-out.

## 2. Lightning AI (lightning.ai/pricing [rendered], /docs/overview/faq/billing [rendered], /docs/platform/build/ai-studio/{auto-sleep,background-execution,interruptible-machines} [rendered])

Credits: "You get 5 free Lightning credits upon registration. Add a card for 25 more. If you don't use them, they expire in 12 months." One-off, not monthly. "1 Lightning AI credit = $1 US dollar"; "Free credits are promotional, non-transferable, and do not accumulate"; purchased credits expire after 1 year. Pro: $50/month monthly or $20/month billed annually; credits "40 monthly / 240 annually". Phone number required. "No credit card" to start.
GPU $/GPU-h, 1-GPU, on-demand (interruptible): T4 16 GB $0.55 ($0.60–0.72); L4 24 GB $0.79 ($0.73–0.87); L40S 48 GB $2.14; RTX PRO 6000 96 GB $2.89 ($3.26–3.91); A100 40 GB $2.19; A100 80 GB $2.71 ($3.66–4.39); H100 $4.68; H200 $4.50 ($3.82–4.58). "Billed by the second." Free limited to "Single GPUs", "Up to 2 concurrent GPUs". "Free GPU hrs with up to 30 free credits to start" (T4 75 h, L4 31 h, L40S 5 h, A100-40 10 h, A100-80 5 h, H100 3 h).
Storage: first 10 GB free, then $0.10/GB/month billed daily; Free and Pro limit 200 GB.
Idle: "Studios automatically sleep after 10 minutes of inactivity"; "If the Studio is running anything such as model training ... it will not go to sleep"; disabling/extending auto-sleep beyond 10 min requires Pro; sleeping Studio costs only storage. Background: "Studios continue to execute code even when the browser window is closed"; notebooks need the tab open. SSH: free card lists "Connect any local IDE or SSH". Session length: footnote "Free Studios run 24/7 but require restart every 4 hours. No restrictions in Pro or higher"; comparison table (Free): "Free active CPU Studio session limit: 4 hours", "T4, L4, L40S session limit: Unlimited", "A100, H100, H200 session limit: 4 hours". Interruptible: two-minute warning; "Upgrade to paid tiers to start using interruptible machines".
Documentation conflicts: (a) headline "GPUs from $0.19/hr" matches no row; (b) interruptible prices for T4, L4, A100-80 listed higher than on-demand while the free card promises "80% off on interruptible (spot)"; (c) free tier: card and FAQ say interruptible usable with free credits, docs say paid tiers only; (d) third-party pages still say "15 monthly credits", which the official FAQ no longer states; (e) 4-hour restart applies to the free CPU Studio; unclear whether a 12-hour L4 run on the free tier survives (table says Unlimited).

## 3. Thunder Compute (thundercompute.com/pricing; /docs/compatibility.md, /docs/billing.md, /docs/guides/stopping-instances.md, /docs/technical-specs, /docs/vscode/operations/snapshots, /docs/console/operations/deleting-instances.md, /docs/restrictions.md, /docs/api-reference/instances/create-instance.md)

A6000: "RTX A6000 48 GB | 6 included · 8 max vCPUs | 48–64 GB RAM | 100–500 GB disk | $0.35 /hr". Extra vCPU $0.04/vCPU-h. L40 $0.79; A100 80 GB $1.09 (8 vCPU incl.); H100 SXM $3.20 (4 vCPU incl.). No T4 or A100-40 GB. Pricing page shows "checked Oct 15, 2026" and "Rates checked Sep 30, 2026". Sandboxes (request access): H100 $2.95/h, A100 $1.95/h, per-second.
Billing: "All compute resources are billed per minute only while your instances run"; payment method required before creating instances; pay via auto-pay card or preloaded credit ("never expires", "non-refundable"); no hard cap — "Threshold charges ... at preset checkpoints (which rise over time) to prevent runaway bills", instance reminder emails, credit-balance alert API. "Instances and sandboxes are never preempted." "If creation fails because a type is sold out, check again."
Stop/snapshot: "Thunder does not have a native 'Stop' feature"; workflow is snapshot -> delete -> restore; "You only pay for snapshot storage while the instance is deleted"; snapshots $0.05/GB/mo "kept until deleted", limit 50, "We do not provide explicit guarantees about snapshot durability", restore "up to 8 minutes per 100GB". Instance disk: first 100 GB per GPU included, $0.219/GB/mo above, "Billed only while running". "Billing stops immediately when an instance is deleted." Egress $0. Conflict: a search-indexed CLI reference page lists `tnr stop`, and a Thunder blog says home directories "survive instance stops"; the current docs guide says no stop exists — treat stop as unavailable.
Compatibility page: "Managed memory / UVM: try a library ... that does not rely on managed memory, such as switching from TensorFlow to PyTorch"; "CUDA Multi-Process Service (MPS)" unsupported; "FFmpeg GPU acceleration" unsupported; "GPU profilers: use application-level logging ... instead". vLLM, bitsandbytes, torch.compile, Triton, flash-attn not mentioned. A previously indexed "Prototyping Mode" page now returns 404; the API marks `mode` "Deprecated". Pre-installed: CUDA 13.0, driver 580, PyTorch 2.9.0+cu128, "Do not attempt to reinstall CUDA". Restrictions: one account per user; B2B/VAT required in some countries.

## 4. Runpod (runpod.io/pricing; docs.runpod.io/pods/pricing, /references/billing-information, /pods/choose-a-pod; getdeploying.com/runpod)

Pricing page (Community prices only in the page's JSON-LD; visible table shows Secure): RTX 4090 Secure $0.89 / Community $0.34; RTX A6000 $0.59 / $0.33; L40S $1.09 / $0.79; RTX 3090 $0.50 / $0.22; RTX A5000 $0.27 / $0.16; L4 $0.59 / $0.44. Availability (getdeploying.com, 2026-10-16T07:00Z): 4090 Community "In stock", Secure "In stock"; A6000 Secure "In stock"; L40S Community "Out of stock", Secure "In stock". Docs: "Runpod is no longer accepting new hosts for Community Cloud. Existing Community Cloud resources remain available."
Billing: per second for compute and storage, no ingress/egress fees (network volumes hourly); prepaid credits, at least one hour's worth to deploy; auto-pay optional; non-refundable; "default spend limit of $80 per hour across all resources" (not a user-settable total cap); at $0 balance pods are stopped (with network volume: data preserved; without: terminated). "Billing runs every 5 minutes."
Stopped storage keeps billing: volume disk $0.10/GB/mo running, $0.20/GB/mo stopped; container disk not charged when stopped (erased); network volume $0.07/GB/mo (<1 TB), $0.05 (>1 TB), high-performance $0.14; "Storage charges continue to accrue on stopped Pods."

## 5. Vast.ai (computeprices.com/providers/vast/gpus/{rtx4090,rtxa6000,l40s} "updated October 16, 2026"; docs.vast.ai/billing; /documentation/instances/choosing/instance-types; /guides/reference/faq/rental-types)

Vast's own /pricing/gpu pages render "No current offers" without JS. Tracker, on-demand per GPU-h: RTX 4090 single-GPU config $0.430 (cheapest config $0.414, 4-GPU); RTX A6000 $0.402 (2-GPU config); L40S $0.800–0.801. Thunder's blog (updated Oct 9, 2026) cites Vast A6000 on-demand $0.46.
Billing: prepaid credits; active rental charged per second while active/connected; storage "$/GB/hr", host-set, "charged for every single second your instance exists, regardless of what state it is in", "Stopping an instance does not avoid storage costs"; bandwidth host-set "$/TB"; nothing charged while Loading or offline; at zero balance instances stop, short grace, then deletion if no card.
Interruptible: bid-priced, "often 50%+ cheaper"; paused if outbid; type cannot be changed after creation.

## 6. Google Colab (research.google.com/colaboratory/faq.html; colab.research.google.com/signup/pricing page source; github.com/googlecolab/google-colab-cli README and skills/colab-operator/SKILL.md; developers.googleblog.com "Introducing the Google Colab CLI" June 5, 2026 and "Colab is now part of your Google AI plan" Sept 22, 2026)

CLI: official, open-source (`uv tool install google-colab-cli`), Linux/macOS; `colab new/run/exec/repl/console/ssh/upload/download/log/stop/usage/pay`; `colab run --gpu T4 train.py` provisions, runs, tears down; GPUs T4, L4, G4, H100, A100; `--high-mem` needs Pro/Pro+. SKILL.md: "Accelerator availability is tier-gated; most accounts can only get CPU"; "`colab new` allocates a billable VM ... idle VMs burn compute units"; a 400 on `colab new` with an accelerator "means no quota/entitlement".
Pricing: Colab Pro $9.99/month "100 compute units per month"; Pro+ $49.99/month 600 total; Pay As You Go $9.99 for 100 CU, $49.99 for 500 CU; "Compute units expire after 90 days"; background execution up to 24 h. No official CU-per-hour table; third-party measurements (March 2026): T4 1.19 CU/h, L4 1.71, A100-40 5.40, A100-80 7.52.
FAQ limits: free "notebooks can run for at most 12 hours"; "access to expensive resources like GPUs is heavily restricted"; disallowed on free runtimes without a positive CU balance: "remote control such as SSH shells, remote desktops", "running distributed computing workers", "bypassing the notebook UI"; Pro+ "continuous code execution for up to 24 hours". Google AI plans (Sept 22, 2026): subscribers get "priority access to faster accelerators".

## 7. Salad (salad.com/pricing; docs.salad.com core-concepts overview, llm-fine-tuning tutorial, Kelpie guide)

RTX 4090 24 GB $/GPU-h by priority: High $0.330, Medium $0.273, Low $0.217, Lowest $0.160; RTX 3090 $0.170 / $0.090 (Lowest). No 48 GB class. vCPU/RAM included; per-second billing; no free trial. High priority: "Never preempted by other workloads. Still subject to node disconnection"; Medium/Low can be preempted after ~30 min.
Requirements: Docker/OCI AMD64 Linux image with a continuously running process; "SaladCloud does not currently support persistent storage"; consumer-owned hardware; "any node running your tasks may shut down unexpectedly"; checkpoint "at least one checkpoint per hour" to R2/S3; Kelpie (free) handles download/upload/resumption; no advance interruption warning documented. Verdict: for a 16–20 GPU-hour pilot of minute-long runs, the plumbing outweighs the ~$1–3 saving.
